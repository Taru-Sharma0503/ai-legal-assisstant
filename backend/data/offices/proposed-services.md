# Office-service coverage review (2026-10-19)

This file records source-backed findings, prototype assumptions and catalogue gaps. Proposed services are not active catalogue entries. Do not reuse a service ID outside its jurisdiction. Verification status belongs to each office-service relationship, not the whole office: `verified` means the cited source establishes the service relationship; `demo_assumed` is only a prototype assumption and must not be presented as officially verified.

## Smallest catalogue proposals (not activated)

Create jurisdiction-scoped records only after the service catalogue schema, IDs, names, and user-facing meaning are reviewed. These are proposals, not seed data. Do not make global service names stand for multiple state-specific legal procedures without preserving jurisdiction.

- **Delhi:** Delhi Income Certificate, Delhi Caste Certificate (SC/ST and OBC may require distinct entries), and Delhi Domicile Certificate. Official evidence: [Income procedure](https://revenue.delhi.gov.in/revenue/income-certificate), [certificate directory](https://revenue.delhi.gov.in/revenue/certificates-faqshelp), [forms](https://revenue.delhi.gov.in/revenue/forms-downloads). Confirm current SDM-area routing before associating the Karol Bagh record. Delhi Birth Certificate already exists, but local-body registrar records are needed for ordinary issuance.
- **Maharashtra / Mumbai City:** Income Certificate, Caste (PR) Certificate, and Residence/Domicile Certificate. Official evidence: [notified services](https://mumbaicity.gov.in/en/services-3), [forms](https://mumbaicity.gov.in/en/forms/). Preserve the distinction between permanent residence/domicile and temporary residence if the catalogue needs both. Associate only with verified relevant ward offices after resolving that district's wards.
- **West Bengal / Kolkata:** Domicile Certificate and Income Certificate, with the Income service restricted to the KMC area as stated by the Collectorate. Official evidence: [citizen services](https://kolkatacollectorate.wb.gov.in/citizen-services/) and [section instructions](https://kolkatacollectorate.wb.gov.in/forms/). The Collectorate source does not establish a Kolkata Caste or Birth Certificate service for this office.
- **Tamil Nadu / Chennai:** Community Certificate, Income Certificate, Nativity Certificate, and Residence Certificate, each kept as its actual local service name rather than conflating Nativity with Domicile. Official evidence: [Chennai e-District services](https://chennai.nic.in/about-district/egovernance/) and [certificate authorities/workflows](https://chennai.nic.in/ta/%E0%AE%9A%E0%AE%BE%E0%AE%A9%E0%AF%8D%E0%AE%B1%E0%AE%BF%E0%AE%A4%E0%AE%B4%E0%AF%8D-%E0%AE%B5%E0%AE%B4%E0%AE%99%E0%AF%8D%E0%AE%95%E0%AF%81%E0%AE%AE%E0%AF%8D-%E0%AE%85%E0%AE%A4%E0%AE%BF%E0%AE%95%E0%AE%BE/). The exact scope and issuing counter for each named taluk need confirmation.
- **Karnataka / Bengaluru Urban:** Caste Certificate and Birth Certificate are directly listed by district pages; a Karnataka Income Certificate is named by the DC office section directory. Official evidence: [Caste](https://bengaluruurban.nic.in/en/service/caste-certificate/), [DC Collectorate sections](https://bengaluruurban.nic.in/en/collectorate/), [Birth](https://bengaluruurban.nic.in/en/service/birth-certificate/). The existing office record is not the local registrar/Nadakacheri location for all these processes; verify a precise service catalogue and office/counter before linking. No Residence/Domicile certificate source was established in this review.

For **Noida**, current UP-scoped catalogue concepts may be usable after the curated office record matches the physical service location. No relationship is added to the City Magistrate record because available certificate pages name Collectorate, G.Noida. Do not add a service solely to increase city coverage.

## Prototype status limitations

`verificationStatus` is currently carried by curated JSON relationships and returned by the local fallback API. `OfficeService` has no persisted status column in the current Prisma schema, so database-backed links return `unknown` and the UI labels them “Status not recorded.” Persisting statuses to the database would require a separately reviewed schema change and migration; none is included here.

## Birth certificate distinction and research limits

Birth registration/certificate issuance is local-body or registrar-specific in the sources reviewed. Delhi's SDM role in delayed registration is an order/appeal workflow, not proof that an SDM office issues the standard certificate. No birth service relationship has been added for any curated office. City coverage remains unsupported where the responsible registrar/local-body office is not a curated record.
